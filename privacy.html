01Our approach

These are personal and hobby/school-assessment projects, not a registered business — as an individual operator we may not be strictly required to comply with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) the way a large organisation is. We've chosen to handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) anyway, because it's the right way to run anything that touches real people's data, including students, robotics teams, and Scout troops.

The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run each feature, we don't sell your data, we don't run ads or third-party trackers, and we tell you here exactly which third parties we do share data with and why.

03How we collect it

  • Directly from you — anything you type into a registration form, profile field, scouting log, notebook entry, AI chat box, or contact form.
  • Automatically — IP address, user agent, and coarse geo (country/city, via Cloudflare's network-level data) are recorded against every Login Panel login attempt and active session, purely for account security (spotting logins from an unexpected location, letting you review/revoke your own active sessions), and kept for a maximum of 30 days before automatic deletion.
  • From third parties — if you sign in via Login Panel's OAuth flow, a connecting site receives only the profile/email fields you consent to on the consent screen; Cloudflare Turnstile provides a pass/fail bot-check signal (see below) rather than sending us personal data about you.

04Why we use it

  • to create and secure your account, and let you sign in across LB Development sites without a separate password everywhere;
  • to provide the actual features you're using — team collaboration, scouting, notebook feedback, AI chat, event lookups;
  • to detect and respond to suspicious logins or abuse (this is what the login-history and session records are for);
  • to respond to contact-form messages and support requests;
  • we do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

05Cookies and similar technology

Login Panel sets a single, essential session cookie (HttpOnly, Secure, scoped to LB Development domains) so you stay signed in — it isn't used for tracking or advertising, and it's deleted when you sign out or expires automatically. Cloudflare Turnstile may set its own cookies as part of verifying you're not a bot on login/registration/forgot-password pages. We don't run Google Analytics, ad pixels, or any other third-party tracking script on any LB Development site.

07Overseas disclosure

Several of the services above (Firebase/Google Cloud, Cloudflare, Cloudinary, Groq, Discord) process data on servers located outside Australia, typically in the United States. By using the Services, you understand your information may be processed overseas, subject to that provider's own security and privacy practices, which you can review on their respective sites.

08AI processing

Pit Wall's AI chat, engineering-notebook feedback, and event analysis send your input — plus relevant context from your own team's robot profile, scouting log, or notebook — to Groq's API to generate a response. This content is processed by a third party to produce that response; don't submit anything you wouldn't want handled that way. We don't use your data to train our own AI models.

09Children and young people

Pit Wall (and, in future, SFOS) are commonly used by people under 18 as part of a school robotics team or Scout troop. We collect the same limited set of information described above regardless of age, and we don't knowingly collect more from a young person than the feature actually needs. We rely on the supervising school, team, or Scout group to obtain any parental/guardian consent appropriate to their own context — this app is a coordination and record-keeping tool, not a substitute for an organisation's child-safety or working-with-children processes.

If you're a parent or guardian and want to review or ask us to delete a young person's data, contact us at help@lbdev.app and we'll work with you directly.

10How long we keep it

  • Login Panel device info — the IP address, browser/device user agent, and coarse country/city recorded against your logins and sessions is kept for a maximum of 30 days, then automatically deleted.
  • Login Panel account deletion removes your profile, active sessions, and connected-app grants from Firebase Authentication and Firestore. A record that a login happened (time, success/fail) is kept even after account deletion, for security and audit purposes, but the device info attached to it is still subject to the 30-day limit above.
  • Pit Wall account deletion removes your own login; your team's shared data (robot profile, scouting log, notebook, chat) stays for your remaining teammates, since it belongs to the team, not any one member.
  • Contact-form submissions persist in our Discord server and inbox like any message you send us, until we or you ask for them to be removed.

11Security

Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication using industry-standard hashing — we never see or store them in plain text. Firestore security rules restrict every team's/user's data to people who belong to it. All Services are served over HTTPS. Login/registration/password-reset are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile against automated abuse. That said, no online service is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of information you transmit to us.

12Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • access a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that's wrong or out of date (most profile fields you can also edit yourself, in Settings);
  • delete your account and associated data, subject to the retention exceptions in section 10 above and any team data that isn't solely yours.

Reach us at help@lbdev.app to exercise any of these. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

13Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your personal information, tell us first at help@lbdev.app so we can try to fix it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

14Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above whenever this changes, and we'll make a reasonable effort to flag material changes in-app or by email — in particular, when SFOS moves from development to a real launch, this policy will be updated with the specifics of what it actually collects before it processes any real user's data.

15Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints about your privacy: help@lbdev.app, or the contact form on lbdev.app.
