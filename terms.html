05Team, troop, and shared group data

Pit Wall (and, in future, SFOS) share data among everyone on the same team: robot profile, scouting notes, chat history, and notebook entries are visible to every member of that team, under their own individual login. Team Roster lets any member edit or remove any other member, with one enforced rule: a team must always keep at least one Owner. We don't mediate disputes between members of the same team about who should have access to what — that's for the team to manage using the roles and roster tools provided.

Only one Pit Wall team can exist per real FTC team number. If you leave a team or delete your own account, the team's shared data stays intact for remaining members.

06AI-powered features

Pit Wall's AI chat, engineering-notebook feedback, and event analysis are generated by a third-party large language model (currently Groq), given context drawn from your own team's data (robot profile, scouting log, notebook entries). AI output is:

  • generated automatically and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or wrong — always sanity-check anything the AI tells you, especially before relying on it for a competition decision or a judged submission;
  • not professional, legal, engineering, or safety advice;
  • not guaranteed to be available at all times (see Third-party services).

Don't paste anything into an AI chat or feedback field that you wouldn't want processed by a third-party AI provider (see the Privacy Policy for exactly how that data is handled).
